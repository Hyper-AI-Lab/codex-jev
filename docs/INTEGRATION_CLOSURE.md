## Research and Limits

[Codex observability](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/config-file/config-advanced)
describes numeric completion telemetry, but installed client shapes must be
validated. [Codex hooks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/hooks) do not intercept
every execution path. [TypeSafe retrieval guidance](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/rerank_typesafe)
describes ranking a supplied shortlist, not compressing the full conversation.
Source histories are inspected locally only; prompts and raw event bodies never
enter measurement storage or hosted ranking requests.
