# Community Conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome different experience levels.
Critique ideas and code, not people. No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
personal-data disclosure, spam or deliberately misleading benchmark claims.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions or limit participation
to protect contributors. Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for harassment; use
the private security reporting route for vulnerabilities or sensitive disclosures.
Do not repost private material to explain a complaint.
